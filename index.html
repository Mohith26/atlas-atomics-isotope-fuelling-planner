Isotope-aware fuelling plan

Assigns each isotope thimble a Lu-177 capsule, a Co-59 stringer or nothing, sets each capsule's irradiation time, and checks the plan against daily on-power refuelling. Every candidate is scored with a two-group diffusion solve of the whole core, so absorber worth, flux shadowing between targets and power peaking are priced in.

Method and assumptions

Core model. 208 pressure-tube channels on a 28.575 cm lattice with a 66 cm D2O reflector, two-group finite-difference diffusion (one node per lattice cell, axial leakage by buckling), 12 bundles per channel with 8-bundle-shift on-power refuelling. Lattice constants follow a representative natural-uranium k-inf(irradiation) curve; the synthetic core is burned in for 640 days to equilibrium (exit burnup MWd/kgU, refuels/FPD).

Planner. Greedy construction, simulated annealing and swap/flip local search over thimble contents, each candidate scored by a full eigenvalue solve: Lu-177 via 176Yb(n,γ) with 177Lu burnout, Co-60 via 59Co(n,γ); reactivity cost converted to discharge-burnup loss through a numerically derived dρ/dω. A loading may not raise peak channel power by more than 0.05 MW over the target-free core.

Verification. Each strategy runs unscored + 42 scored full-power days of daily channel selection by pre-simulation (each candidate refuel is solved before it is chosen) against the reference power map. Prices and capsule cost are illustrative inputs, not market data. This is a planning sketch, not a licensed core-physics code.
